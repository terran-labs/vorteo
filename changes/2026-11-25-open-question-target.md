## Maintenance review

Issue #660. The daemon records the thread's latest timeline position on a new question as optional `askedSeq` and `askedEpoch` (wire-compatible; older questions have none and show the notice). A position from another timeline epoch is never matched, because a rebuilt timeline reuses sequence numbers. The app keeps a one-shot conversation target, highlights the targeted question, and jumps to the item ending at or just before that position within a contiguous loaded range, fetching older history otherwise through the same fetch the chat outline uses. Whether the place is missing is decided from the fetch result, not a timer; the jump is consumed once it lands or is reported, a reader's scroll cancels it, and a target that expires unconsumed is reported. Tests: the question model records the position and epoch, opening a question records a target, the position rules reject another epoch and an unloaded gap, and one browser case covers a jump that must fetch older history, renders late, ignores another epoch and yields to a reader's scroll. No inventory entry changes; README overview and onboarding are unchanged.
