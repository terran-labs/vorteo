### Fixes

- A queued message that is open for editing is no longer sent while you edit it. The daemon holds it, and the messages queued behind it, until you save or cancel the edit, for at most two minutes after the editor last checks in, so a closed window cannot block the queue for long. Other windows show "Being edited" on the message and refuse to edit it or send it now with "This message is being edited in another window." On an older host that cannot hold messages, opening the editor first warns "This message may be sent as it is while you edit it."

## Maintenance review

Issue #691, part 2 (part 1, the recovery wording, is a separate change). Queue items gain optional `editHold` (`holderId`, `expiresAt`), and queue mutations gain `hold_edit` (acquire or renew, with `expectedRevision` and `holderId`) and `release_edit`, gated on the new `messageQueueEditHold` daemon feature. The store sets expiry two minutes after each renewal, refuses another holder's unexpired hold and send now on a held item with `delivery_conflict`, releases the hold atomically with an edit, keeps the item revision unchanged, and records no receipt or ingress evidence for hold operations. Delivery skips a held head and everything behind it, both before admission and inside the claim, and schedules a wake at expiry; held items are persisted, so a restarted daemon keeps them until expiry. The #680 size refusal is unchanged. The app uses the draft identity as holder, shared by tabs showing the same draft; it acquires before creating the draft, renews every 30 seconds while the editor shows a current queued revision, releases on cancel or close and not on save, sends holds directly rather than through the durable outbox, which is now typed to content operations only, and refuses send now locally on a held item. Tests: one delivery case (held head and the message behind it wait, survive a restarted store, refuse another holder and send now, and deliver after expiry through the scheduled wake) and one draft session case (open sends `hold_edit`; save sends no release); each assertion fails with its fix reverted. The protocol compatibility guide and the recoverable local queue edits inventory entry are updated. README overview and onboarding are unchanged.
