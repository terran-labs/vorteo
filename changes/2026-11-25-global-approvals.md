### Improvements

- A thread stopped on a tool approval that no supervising thread can answer now shows in the sidebar's Open questions card: "<thread> is stopped until you answer.", the thread's mode, its working directory, the reason, and the command. Accept appears once the full command has been expanded; until then the card offers Open thread, which lands on the approval. Deny is one tap. ## Maintenance review

Issue #659, part C. The daemon records each thread's creator as `createdByAgentId` when the thread is created, from the parent label as it stands at creation, which `create_agent` always sets to the authenticated caller and strips for detached threads. Later label edits never change it, no snapshot or record write can replace it,. A thread nobody created, or created before this change, stores no creator, so existing records are unchanged and their approvals go to the owner. Snapshots carry it behind the new `agentCreatedBy` capability; older hosts show no approvals on the card, as before. An approval surfaces when the thread has no creator, or when no ancestor up its creator chain can answer (closed, archived, missing, or itself waiting on an approval). Pending permissions record the timeline position and epoch where they were requested, so Open thread lands on them and a position from a rebuilt timeline is reported as not found. Tests: the routing table, and the storage rules for the creator. No inventory entry changes; README overview and onboarding are unchanged.
