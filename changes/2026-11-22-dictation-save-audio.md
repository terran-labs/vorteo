### Features

- A failed dictation offers Save audio, which saves the recording as a WAV file without deleting it: through the share sheet on iPhone and the installed web app, as a download in other browsers, and through the share sheet in the native app.

### Fixes

- A failed dictation says whether its recording is kept on this device or, when device storage failed, that it is not saved and the page should stay open until it is sent.
- A notice appears when this device cannot store recordings, and when an older unsent recording is removed to free space.
- Starting dictation waits for the microphone to finish stopping, so a new recording cannot overlap the end of the previous one.
