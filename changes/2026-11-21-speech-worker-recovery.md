### Fixes

- Local dictation no longer stalls behind its own transcription. The on-device recognizer used to decode inside each audio message on the speech worker's only thread, so on a busy machine every later message and every new dictation start waited for that decode, and timed out after 30 seconds. Decoding now runs off the worker's main thread, one segment at a time, with finished segments transcribed before live previews. The worker keeps answering while a decode takes a minute or more.
- Live previews use at most a third of the recognizer's time and are skipped while a recording is replayed faster than real time, so a retried long recording only pays for its final transcription.
- Dictation sessions report progress (segments decoded, segments and audio still pending) after each segment and every 5 seconds while a segment decodes, so the dictation manager can keep waiting while work continues. Other local speech requests fail only after the worker has sent nothing for 60 seconds, and such a silent worker is replaced.
- A dictation or voice start on an unresponsive local speech worker replaces that worker and retries once, instead of reusing it for every later start. Starts wait at most 8 seconds on a warm worker and 20 seconds for the first start after a worker or model loads, and the whole start, retry included, finishes within 28 seconds, before the app's own 30 second wait. A worker serving other sessions is never replaced for a slow start unless it has been silent for 60 seconds. A session created after its start gave up is closed instead of leaking.
- Whisper models no longer drop audio beyond 30 seconds in one segment. A longer segment, which happens when dictation auto-commit is disabled or set above 30 seconds, is split at its quietest moment before the window and transcribed in full.
- A recognizer decode that never finishes no longer hides behind progress reports. Each decode is bounded at four times its audio length, and never less than two minutes. Past that, progress stops, the session fails with `speech_worker_timeout`, and the worker exits so the next dictation gets a fresh one.
- Local speech failures carry stable codes for the error display: `speech_worker_timeout`, `speech_worker_exited`, `speech_unavailable`, and `speech_decode_failed` for errors the recognizer reports during a session. Messages are unchanged.
